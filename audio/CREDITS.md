## Processing

Each source `.ogg` was trimmed of leading silence, loudness-normalized, downmixed to
mono, resampled to 22.05 kHz and encoded as MP3 at ~64-96 kbps (ffmpeg). Total size
of all 12 files is under 100 KB.
